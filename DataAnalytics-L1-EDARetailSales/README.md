# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Objective
Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer behaviour patterns, product performance, and business insights.

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed
- Initial dataset inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer demographics analysis
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Additional visualization for business insights

## Key Insights
- Identified important sales trends over time.
- Analysed the performance of top-selling products.
- Compared revenue across product categories.
- Examined relationships between numerical variables.
- Derived actionable business insights from the analysis.
